**About Segula Technologies**

**Overview**
Segula Technologies is a global engineering group founded in 1999. With headquarters in Levallois-Perret, France, we have established ourselves as a premier engineering and technology consulting firm. Our mission is to "Make tomorrow's industry" through engineering excellence, sustainable practices, and continuous innovation.

**Global Reach**
We employ over 15,000 highly qualified engineers and specialists worldwide, operating in more than 30 countries. This international presence allows us to partner closely with major industrial players everywhere they operate.

**Key Industries**
Segula specializes in complex product engineering, manufacturing processes, and digital transformation across several core sectors:
- **Automotive**: We work on electric vehicle (EV) platforms, autonomous driving systems, and connected car technologies.
- **Aerospace & Defense**: From aerostructures to defense systems and avionics.
- **Energy**: Specializing in green energy, nuclear, and offshore wind farms.
- **Rail & Naval**: Infrastructure, signaling, and large vessel engineering.
- **Life Sciences**: Medical devices and pharmaceutical engineering.
- **Oil & Gas**: Refining processes and pipeline engineering.

**Innovation Hubs**
A significant portion of our revenue is reinvested into our internal R&D centers. Our main innovation labs focus on:
- Digital Twin technology and Digital Factories
- Artificial Intelligence and Predictive Maintenance
- Green mobility and hydrogen-powered vehicles

**Career Pathways**
Segula promotes continuous learning. We offer a structured training catalog and require employees to complete a minimum of 40 hours of training per year. Promotions are tracked via our annual 360-degree feedback loops.

**Application Process**
To join our team:
1. Upload your CV via our HR Portal to trigger our AI Candidate-Job Matching system.
2. If your score exceeds 70%, you will be invited for an initial 30-minute screening call with HR.
3. Successful candidates proceed to a technical interview with the hiring manager.
4. The final stage is a cultural fit interview with the department head.

**Segula Technologies – Employee Benefits Full Guide**

At Segula Technologies, we prioritize the well-being and growth of our employees. Our comprehensive benefits package applies to all full-time employees from day one of employment.

1. **Health & Wellness**
   - **Health Insurance**: 100% premium covered for employees, and 50% for dependents. Includes full medical, dental, and vision coverage.
   - **Mental Health**: Free access to 24/7 confidential counseling services through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP).
   - **Fitness Area**: On-site gyms at major hubs (Paris, Lyon) and fitness subsidies (€30/month) for remote workers or local offices without gyms.

2. **Compensation & Rewards**
   - **Competitive Salary**: Market-aligned compensation, reviewed annually every January to keep up with inflation and performance.
   - **Performance Bonuses**: Annual bonuses distributed in March, based 50% on personal KPIs and 50% on company performance.
   - **Referral Program**: €1,000 bonus for referring a candidate who is successfully hired and passes their 3-month probation period.

3. **Leave Policy**
   - **Annual Leave**: 20 days per year. Unused leave can be carried forward (up to 5 days) to the next year.
   - **Sick Leave**: 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required if the absence exceeds 3 consecutive days.
   - **Emergency Leave**: 3 days per year, with same-day approval available in the HR portal.
   - **Parental Leave**: 90 days for maternity leave and 14 days for paternity leave, fully paid.

4. **Remote Work & Mobility**
   - **Hybrid Work**: Up to 2 days of remote work per week, subject to direct manager approval.
   - **Transportation**: 50% reimbursement on monthly public transport passes, or free on-site parking depending on the location.
   - **Mobility**: We operate in over 30 countries. After 2 years, employees can request international transfers.
